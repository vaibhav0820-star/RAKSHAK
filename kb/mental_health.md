## Panic attack, extreme anxiety and shock
tags: panic attack, anxiety, hyperventilating, rapid breathing, shaking, fear, terrified, trauma, crying, panic, mental health
1. Guide their breathing with Box Breathing: Inhale slowly through the nose for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, exhale slowly through the mouth for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds. Repeat 5 times.
2. Speak in a calm, low, firm voice: "You are safe right now. I am here with you. Focus on my voice."
3. Use the 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding method: Ask them to name 5 things they see, 4 things they can feel, 3 things they can hear, 2 things they can smell, and 1 positive affirmation.
4. If hyperventilating (breathing very fast): Have them cup their hands loosely around mouth and nose or breathe with pursed lips. Do not use a paper bag if heart or lung disease is possible.
5. Keep them warm with a blanket or jacket. Shock often causes shivering and feeling cold.
6. For severe acute mental distress or suicidal thoughts in India, call Tele-MANAS at 14416 (24/7 free) or 112.

## Psychological first aid in disaster
tags: shock, psychological first aid, trauma, grief, overwhelmed, disaster stress, scared children
1. Look: Ensure immediate safety, check for physical injuries, and protect from further trauma or sight of severe casualties.
2. Listen: Approach calmly, introduce yourself, ask what they need most, and listen without interrupting or giving false promises.
3. Link: Connect families together, provide clean drinking water, food, and private shelter.
4. For children: Speak at eye level, keep them close to trusted family, give simple honest facts, and provide comfort items.
